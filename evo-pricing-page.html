Choose how you use Evo-English

Evo-English is a learning platform for independent students and English teachers. Teachers pay for platform tools. Lesson payments between teachers and students are arranged separately outside Evo-English.

Existing accounts receive a 14-day free access period starting May 25, 2026. The free period ends on June 8, 2026. No user is charged during the free period unless they actively start a paid subscription.
What people pay for
  • Teacher workspace, assignments, templates and review tools.
  • Vocabulary card modules and student practice areas.
  • Self-study access for independent learners.
  • Students linked to a teacher use teacher-assigned materials for free.

Payment, cancellation and free access

Subscriptions are monthly, renew automatically after paid activation, and can be canceled before the next billing period.

Billing period

All paid plans are monthly subscriptions. After a user starts a paid subscription and authorizes card payments, the subscription renews automatically every month until canceled.

Free period

Existing users receive free access from May 25, 2026 until June 8, 2026. A card is not charged during the free period unless the user actively starts a paid subscription.

Teacher-linked students

Students connected to a teacher do not pay Evo-English for teacher-assigned homework, cards or feedback areas.

Cancellation

Users may cancel before the next billing period. Cancellation stops the next renewal, and access remains available until the end of the already paid period.

Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Please contact support within 14 days of payment and include your account email and payment details.

Contact

For payment, subscription or account questions, contact Evo-English by email: evoenglish@outlook.com.

Important: Evo-English subscription payments are for platform access only. Evo-English does not collect or process private lesson payments between teachers and their students.
